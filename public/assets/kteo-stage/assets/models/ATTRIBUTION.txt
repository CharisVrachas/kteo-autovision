Changes made: the Rigacar rig was removed, modifiers were applied, the car was
turned to face +Y and stood on the origin, the 134 loose objects were grouped
into the dozen the page drives, each wheel's origin was moved onto its hub, the
bonnet's origin was moved onto its hinge, and the textures were resized for the
web. No geometry was modelled or removed — the bonnet, doors, brake discs,
calipers, engine, exhaust, suspension, interior and lamps are all the author's.

CC BY 4.0 requires that this credit travels with the work. It is kept here, next
to the model, rather than only in the project README, because the folder this
sits in is meant to be copied wholesale into a site — and a credit left behind in
a README that did not get copied is a licence breach.

Put the credit somewhere a visitor can reach it: a colophon, an "about" page, or
a line in the page footer. Something like:

    3D model: "Generic Sedan Car" by MMC Works, CC BY 4.0

A note on why this model and not a nicer-looking one. Most free car models of
this quality are of REAL, branded cars — the mesh may be CC-BY, but the car's
design is the manufacturer's and is protected separately, whichever licence the
modeller attached. This one is a generic design, which is what makes it safe to
put on a commercial page.
